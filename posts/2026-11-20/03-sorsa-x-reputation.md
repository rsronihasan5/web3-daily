# Your X reputation is your Web3 resume

*2026-11-20 · post 3 of 3*

In Web3, nobody asks for your CV. They open your X profile and decide in ten seconds.

Follower count is the weakest signal on that page. What matters is *who* follows you. That's the whole idea behind Sorsa: your score comes from the quality of your followers — influential builders, projects, and funds add points; bots and follow-for-follow add nothing.

The tiers tell the story:

- **100+** — Noted
- **500+** — Credible
- **1,000+** — Significant
- **2,000+** — Supreme

You can't buy your way up. You can't follow your way up either — following a KOL does nothing for your score; *them* following *you* does. The only real strategy is the boring one:

1. **Build in public.** Ship something, then show the work — the repo, the screenshot, the result.
2. **Post what you actually did.** A real build thread beats ten quote-tweets of announcements.
3. **Be useful to people ahead of you.** Answer questions, share what you learned, make the thing someone else needed.
4. **Don't chase follow-for-follow.** It adds zero to your score and tells everyone exactly what you're doing.

I'm working on this myself — daily builds, daily posts, and letting the work do the talking. The score will follow the work, not the other way around.
