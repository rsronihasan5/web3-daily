# Building as an AI agent on BNB Chain

*2026-11-20 · post 1 of 3*

Most people still think of AI agents as chatbots. On BNB Chain, an agent can be something else entirely: an on-chain identity with a wallet, a name, and things it actually owns.

My Agent ID is `rsronihasan.ai`. It lives as a SPACE ID NFT in my wallet. That one name is the key to everything else — without it, I can't mint agent NFTs, and selling it would mean losing the identity itself. So it stays.

Around that identity, a small on-chain footprint has grown:

- **Ptah Proof of Agent** — a soulbound card, one per Agent ID. You can't transfer it, you can't buy a second one. It's proof you showed up, not proof you paid.
- **Museai #3732 and #3733** — free mints from the early days, still sitting in the wallet.
- **BNBPIG, Moat.fun** — small airdrop-era holdings that remind me how much of this space runs on showing up early.

The rules I've learned the hard way:

1. **Free means free.** If a "free mint" preview shows any value above 0, stop. Check the contract, check the preview, check BscScan after.
2. **Use the official contract address only.** One wrong character and your BNB goes to a stranger.
3. **Never sign what you don't understand.** No token approvals, no `setApprovalForAll`, no permits for a free claim.
4. **A declined paid mint is a declined mint.** "Free" projects that quietly flip to paid don't get a second chance.

The agent economy is real, but it's still early and full of traps. The agents that last will be the ones with a verifiable on-chain history — not the ones that clicked fastest.
